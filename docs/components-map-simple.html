Event Scout: Components Map

Finds industry events for Vikram, ranks them, flags deadlines and emails him every Monday.
Live app: event-scout-five.vercel.app
Code: github.com/vyombhatnagar-lgtm/event-scout
1
Event Sources
Eventbrite, Luma, Meetup and AllEvents, plus any event link Vikram pastes in.
Why: events are scattered across many sites, so we collect them in one place.
→
2
Discovery Engine
Fetches events every day, cleans them into one format and removes duplicates.
Why: the same event is often listed on two or three sites.
→
3
Deadline Check
Reads the registration closing date from the event page. Under 7 days is marked urgent.
Why: Vikram's main problem was missing deadlines. We never guess a date.
↓
6
Database (Neon)
Stores events, scores, Vikram's preferences and his tasks.
Why: preferences can change without touching code, and nothing is lost between weeks.
←
5
Ranking
Final score = 35% Vikram's preferences (cities, topics) + 65% Gemini's score.
Why: fixed rules keep it consistent; AI adds judgement about the audience.
←
4
AI: Gemini Flash
Summarises each event and gives a fit score from 0 to 100 with a one line reason.
Why: required by the brief. AI output is checked against the source text.
↓
7
Scheduler (Vercel Cron)
Runs discovery daily at 5:00 AM and sends the briefing every Monday at 6:30 AM.
Why: it runs on its own, so Vikram does not have to do anything.
→
8
Monday Email (Resend)
Urgent deadlines first, then the best matches, each with register and calendar links.
Why: replaces his 45 minutes of manual searching every Monday.
→
9
Dashboard (Next.js on Vercel)
Today view, Explore, My events and Settings. Save, register, add to calendar, track tasks.
Why: one place to decide quickly and follow up through the week.
Built with Next.js · Neon · Gemini Flash · Resend · Vercel · GitHubAll AI in the app uses Gemini Flash only

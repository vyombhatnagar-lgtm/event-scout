Event Scout — Components Map

Problem statement: Industry Event Scout
Live app: event-scout-five.vercel.app
Repo: github.com/vyombhatnagar-lgtm/event-scout

Vikram, an independent consultant, wins 40% of new business in rooms with decision-makers but loses ~45 minutes every Monday hunting fragmented listings — and still misses deadlines. Event Scout pulls live events from many sources, ranks them for his focus areas with Gemini Flash, flags registrations closing within 7 days, and delivers a Monday 06:30 IST briefing plus a daily “Today” view.
